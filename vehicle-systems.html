Vehicle systems we support

System-level visibility across the entire commercial-vehicle stack.

Engine systems

Genealogy gaps arise when engine performance history is fragmented across service events and platform generations. Duty-cycle context helps explain wear, efficiency, and condition changes. OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers need visibility into recurring patterns, not isolated faults. BodhWorks helps reconstruct the long-running operating story behind engine behavior.

Transmission systems

Transmission performance is often obscured by incomplete service history and differing duty patterns. Real-world usage context is essential to distinguish design limitations, misuse, and component stress. BodhWorks helps create a consistent view across transmission configurations and operating conditions.

Aftertreatment systems

Aftertreatment performance depends on duty cycle, operating environment, and compliance context. Gaps often emerge when emissions-related events are analyzed without the full usage narrative. OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers need consistent, traceable insight into how these systems are being used. BodhWorks helps connect the evidence.

Braking and chassis systems

Damage, wear, and degradation are not fully understandable without an operating context that accounts for load, terrain, and duty pattern. The challenge is often not the single brake event but the sequence of conditions that produced it. BodhWorks helps reveal that continuity.

ADAS and safety systems

ADAS and safety systems depend on calibration context, usage patterns, and system interaction. Gaps occur when individual alerts are reviewed without the broader vehicle context. BodhWorks helps connect usage-driven behavior with system integrity and operational performance.

Electric powertrain systems

Electric powertrain behavior is shaped by operational load, charging patterns, thermal exposure, and duty regime. Without context, faults can be misread or over-weighted. OEMs and suppliers need a consistent way to evaluate these systems in real-world conditions. BodhWorks provides cross-system continuity.

Telematics and connectivity systems

Connectivity and telemetry often provide the most fragmented view, because device behavior, signal quality, and vehicle data flows differ sharply across fleets. Duty-cycle intelligence matters here because connectivity is only valuable when its operational context is understood. BodhWorks helps unify that context across platforms and stakeholders.
