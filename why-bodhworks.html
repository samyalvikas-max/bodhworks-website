Why BodhWorks

Industry transformation needs a neutral view.

The problem

The commercial-vehicle ecosystem is fragmented at the exact moment it needs more clarity.

Vehicle data is spread across OEM portals, telematics systems, service records, component histories, and operational workflows. Each participant sees a fragment of the story, but few can connect the full picture across time, systems, and stakeholders.

What is happening
Fragmented telematics

Different systems generate different truths.

Telematics data, service events, and vehicle health signals often live in separate chains of custody and are not shared in a common frame of reference.

Siloed portals

OEM views are useful, but incomplete.

Portal access may help teams understand one platform at a time, yet it does not capture cross-OEM context or mixed-fleet complexity in a neutral way.

Blind spots

Component-level signals are not enough.

Important events often appear as isolated faults, missing the broader lineage, duty cycle, and operating environment that explain their significance.

The operating reality
Mixed fleets

One fleet can contain many generations and many realities.

Commercial operations routinely combine different OEMs, system configurations, duty patterns, and maintenance approaches under one operating banner.

Regulatory pressure

Compliance requires traceability and context.

As regulations become more demanding, the need for coherent evidence and system-level context becomes essential across the ecosystem.

Cross-system intelligence

Meaning comes from relationships, not isolated alerts.

A robust view must connect engines, transmissions, braking, aftertreatment, ADAS, electric powertrain, and telemetry into one operating narrative.
