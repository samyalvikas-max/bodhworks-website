Vision

Commercial vehicles are becoming more connected, more regulated, and more complex.

The challenge is no longer simply collecting data. It is understanding how vehicles, systems, components, and duty cycles relate across OEM boundaries, powertrains, and operational environments. BodhWorks is designed to provide that system-level continuity without bias toward any single platform or supplier.

Why the industry needs a neutral layer
Fragmented reality

Vehicle intelligence is fragmented across brands and platforms.

Data exists in silos, and the context needed to interpret it is often lost before it reaches the teams accountable for performance and reliability.

Siloed ownership

OEM portals do not tell the whole story.

Even within a single fleet, each stakeholder sees only part of the operating picture. Knowledge is distributed, not unified.

Cross-system complexity

Mixed fleets need more than isolated alerts.

A vehicle can contain multiple generations of systems, multiple powertrain types, and multiple governance environments. The operating story must be reconstructed as a whole.

Overview

BodhWorks reconstructs vehicle context across the ecosystem.

It is designed to bring together genealogy reconstruction, duty-cycle intelligence, and cross-system visibility in a way that is resilient to market fragmentation and future platform change. Whether the vehicle is ICE, hybrid, or electric, the goal remains the same: create a trusted understanding of how a system has lived, how it is being used, and how it should be evaluated.
