Our approach

A practical starting point for vehicle-system intelligence.

MVP

BodhWorks begins with system-agnostic warranty leakage detection.

This is a workflow that can apply to any major vehicle system, regardless of OEM, powertrain, or operating context. It starts with the question that matters most: where is evidence weak, context incomplete, or the operating story inconsistent?

The workflow
Duty-cycle reconstruction

Understand how the vehicle was actually used.

Real-world duty cycles are reconstructed into a usable context that can explain why a system behaved the way it did.

Fault lineage mapping

Trace the path from symptom to cause.

Fault events are connected across time, system boundaries, and operational context so the evidence tells a coherent story instead of a fragmented one.

Cross-OEM context

Compare against the broader operating landscape.

The workflow brings in comparable context without forcing a single platform viewpoint, helping stakeholders interpret behavior with greater clarity.

Evidence generation

Produce defensible, reviewable insight.

Evidence is compiled in a form that supports human review, adjudication, and cross- functional understanding without relying on opaque black-box logic.

Human-gated workflow

Keep judgment in the loop.

BodhWorks is designed to support human decision-making, not replace it. Review, validation, and accountability remain central to the workflow.

No forced integration

Designed for authorized operational workflows.

The approach supports the realities of mixed systems and governed access without requiring disruptive or heavy system integration at the outset.
