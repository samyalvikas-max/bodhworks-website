Roadmap

A long-term architecture for neutral vehicle intelligence.

Future-proof expansion

The BodhWorks roadmap extends from a strong MVP to a broad neutral operating layer.

The platform is designed to expand across domains, use cases, and stakeholder groups while staying anchored to the same fundamental principles: neutrality, governance, system context, and operational trust.

Transmission genealogy

Reconstruct transmission lineage with usage context.

Extend the same neutral logic used for broader vehicle intelligence to transmission architecture and lifecycle interpretation.

Aftertreatment compliance intelligence

Bring regulatory context into operational understanding.

Connect duty cycle, compliance history, and emissions-related system behavior in a more coherent and reviewable format.

ADAS calibration drift analytics

Support safety-system context over time.

Track shifts in calibration relevance, operating conditions, and system drift as part of a broader safety and reliability narrative.

EV battery duty-cycle intelligence

Support future powertrain complexity with better usage understanding.

Help connect battery condition, duty pattern, and operational context in a way that is consistent with the broader system-view strategy.

Mixed-fleet uptime intelligence

Connect operational resilience across brands and systems.

Expand from isolated system analysis to fleet-wide understanding that supports uptime, maintenance, and asset strategy decisions.

Full neutral intelligence layer

Unify OEM, Tier-1, and operator context.

Over time, BodhWorks is intended to become a neutral intelligence layer spanning the full ecosystem of vehicle systems, stakeholders, and operating realities.
