Neutral intelligence layer

A trusted view across the full vehicle ecosystem.

Core idea

Neutrality is not neutrality without governance.

BodhWorks is built as a governed layer that can unify evidence, lineage, and usage context across multiple OEMs, suppliers, and operating models without favoring one system over another. It supports decision-making with clarity and traceability while respecting ownership, confidentiality, and commercial boundaries.

What neutrality means
System-agnostic

It is not tied to a single engine, platform, or supplier architecture.

It is designed to accommodate the realities of mixed fleets, multi-powertrain operations, and evolving regulations instead of forcing a single vendor lens.

Protected IP

Confidentiality remains intact.

The layer is built to preserve the proprietary interests of OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, and operators while still enabling a shared operational understanding.

Contextual access

Shared insight does not mean shared exposure.

Governance ensures that relevant evidence is available to the right stakeholders in the right context, without unnecessary disclosure.

How it works
Governance

Data is governed with intent.

BodhWorks is designed around clear stewardship, permissions, and role-aware access so evidence can be used responsibly across stakeholders.

Genealogy

Vehicle and system history is reconstructed.

Through lineage mapping and operational context, BodhWorks helps teams understand how a system arrived at its current condition and how that condition should be interpreted.

Operator-authorized workflows

Integration without forced integration.

The model is designed to work through authorized, controlled workflows that respect operational realities and do not require disruptive system changes.

Why it matters

Mixed-fleet context is not an add-on. It is a strategic requirement.

In a world where vehicles operate across multiple OEM ecosystems, duty patterns, and regulatory environments, intelligent decisions require a connection between the asset, the system, and the operating context. BodhWorks reconstructs this continuity in a form that is accessible to the real business and operational stakeholders involved.
